# Discovery notes: Lumen Med Spa (fictional)

The discovery record for the capstone engagement. Everything here is as the client stated it during discovery. Volumes and patterns are the client's statements, recorded as stated, and the whole engagement is a worked example for teaching.

## Channels and volumes, as stated

- Channels: Instagram direct messages, WhatsApp, and website enquiry form, across three locations.
- Volume as stated by the owner: roughly 45 to 60 enquiries a week across all locations and channels.
- Timing as stated: a large share arrives in the evenings and at weekends. The owner estimated about half outside front desk hours.

## Current response pattern, as stated

- First replies during staffed hours usually land within the hour when the desk is quiet, and slip to several hours when it is busy.
- Evenings and weekends wait until the next staffed period in most cases.
- Follow-up after an enquiry goes quiet is ad hoc: a staff member sometimes messages once, with no sequence and no record.
- Booking is manual: staff check the diary and confirm by message, retyping details the enquiry already provided.

## The value anchor, in the client's own framing

The owner framed the value without prompting: even a small number of recovered bookings a month matters at their average treatment value, and the larger cost they named was reputational, enquiries that conclude the business is not responsive. No figure from this framing is used as a promise anywhere in the engagement. It is recorded because Lesson 14 prices against the client's own anchor, stated in their words, and because it explains why response time, not feature count, leads the scope.

## Boundaries and exclusions, agreed before pricing

- The system answers, qualifies, books, and follows up. Clinical advice is out of scope: treatment suitability questions go to a human.
- Anyone who opts out is never messaged again by the sequence. This is a hard rule, and Lesson 30's QA gate tests it.
- The price list and service descriptions are supplied by the client and treated as the source of truth. The system never invents a price.
- Advertising, social content, and price list changes are excluded, as the brief states.
- Data touched: enquiry text, contact details, and booking preferences. Nothing else is collected.

## Open items recorded honestly

- Diary access for the booking step was still being arranged by the client's side at pricing time. The build plan in Lesson 30 sequences around it.
- Weekend staffing intentions were undecided. The system is scoped to work either way.
