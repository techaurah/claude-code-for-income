# Client brief: Lumen Med Spa (fictional)

Written in the client's own words, lightly tidied, as the capstone engagement received it. Lumen Med Spa is a fictional three-location med spa group invented for this course.

## Who we are

We run three med spa locations. Our treatments are booked days ahead when we answer fast, and lost quietly when we do not.

## What happens now

Enquiries arrive on Instagram, on WhatsApp, and through the website. A large share arrives in the evenings and at weekends, when the front desk is thinnest. Our front desk team is stretched across the three locations, and first replies can take hours. By the time we reply, many people have booked somewhere else or gone quiet, and we rarely follow up in any organised way.

## What we want

Replies in minutes at all hours, on all three channels. Enquiries qualified against our real services and price list, so nobody is quoted the wrong treatment or the wrong price. Bookings written into the diary without the front desk retyping anything. A diary that fills itself, within the rules we set. And a follow-up sequence for enquiries that go quiet, because our own numbers tell us a share of them were still deciding.

## What we are buying, as scoped

Scope lines for this engagement, matching the price build-up in engagement.json:

- Discovery and scope: confirm channels, volumes, services, price list, and diary rules, and record them before any building starts.
- Build: the enquiry response system across Instagram, WhatsApp, and the website, with qualification against our services and price list, booking into the diary, and the follow-up sequence for quiet enquiries.
- QA and hardening: test the system against our real enquiry patterns, including out of hours, opt-outs, and questions it should hand to a human.
- Handover and training: show our team how the system works, what it will not do, and how to take over a conversation.
- Support window: 30 days of support after handover for defects and small corrections within the agreed scope.

## What we are not asking for

No advertising, no social content, no changes to our price list itself, and no promises about how many bookings this creates. We are buying response speed and follow-through. We will judge it on replies, bookings, and the enquiries we stop losing.
