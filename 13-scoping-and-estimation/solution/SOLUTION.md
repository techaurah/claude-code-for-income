# Solution for Lesson 13: Scoping and estimation when an agent does the typing

The shipped `lumen_scope.json` is the worked scope. Human hours sum to 9: discovery 2, build 3, booking integration 2, QA and handover prep 2. Agent hours sum to 13. Buffers follow the classes exactly: low tasks add 10 percent of 4 human hours, 0.40 hours, med adds 25 percent of 3 hours, 0.75, and high adds 50 percent of 2 hours, 1.00, for a total buffer of 2.15 hours and a human total with buffer of 11.15.

Note where the high class landed: the booking integration, the one task whose shape an external calendar controls. That placement is the discovery lesson working as intended. The exclusions that belong under this scope include a second messaging channel, a fourth location, migration of past enquiries, and writing the spa's treatment copy, with two revision rounds on the delivered scope included.
