# Capstone grading rubric

Grade each criterion from 0 to 3 against its descriptors. Grade the evidence in the pack, not the effort behind it and not the intention. A criterion with no evidence in the pack scores 0, however good the underlying work was.

## 1. Scope fidelity (weight 0.20)

- 0: The pack delivers a different engagement from the brief, or scope lines are missing.
- 1: Most scope lines are delivered, but at least one is silently dropped or swapped.
- 2: Every scope line in the brief is delivered and traceable to evidence.
- 3: Every scope line is delivered and traceable, and exclusions are restated with evidence that they were honoured.

## 2. Price integrity (weight 0.15)

- 0: The charged price cannot be rebuilt from the build-up, or figures disagree between documents.
- 1: The price rebuilds, but assumptions are missing or the margin is quoted without them.
- 2: The build-up sums to the price, the floor sums to its parts, and assumptions are printed with the numbers.
- 3: As 2, and any variance between estimate and measured cost is printed and explained, not absorbed.

## 3. QA evidence (weight 0.20)

- 0: No QA record exists, or it claims a clean run with no check list behind it.
- 1: A check list exists, but results are summary claims without per-check records.
- 2: Every check is recorded with a result, and failures carry a fix and a retest.
- 3: As 2, and the checks trace back to brief lines and discovery risks, including at least one check that protects a person.

## 4. Handover completeness (weight 0.15)

- 0: No handover material. The client cannot run or explain the system.
- 1: Partial notes exist, but training, limits, or the take-over path is missing.
- 2: Handover notes, a runbook, and training are all present, with the system's limits stated.
- 3: As 2, and the pack shows the client team actually used the material (a walked session, questions answered, or a signed receipt).

## 5. Honesty of numbers (weight 0.20)

- 0: Figures are invented, unsourced, or presented as promises or typical outcomes.
- 1: Figures are real but stripped of their context, fees, or assumptions.
- 2: Every figure is sourced platform data with limits, a dated public price, or a labelled worked example with assumptions.
- 3: As 2, and the pack names what the numbers do not show, such as outcomes the system cannot claim credit for.

## 6. Support readiness (weight 0.10)

- 0: No support terms. The client does not know what happens when something breaks.
- 1: A support window is named, but scope, response times, or the end date is unstated.
- 2: The support window has a stated scope, response expectations, and an end date, and the retainer path after it is described.
- 3: As 2, and the first report cycle is already scheduled, with the scope list the Lesson 26 report will check against.

## Verdict bands

Scores are weighted by rubric_score.py into a percentage of the maximum.

- 85 to 100: Ship it.
- 60 to 84: Fix and reship. Name the criteria holding the pack back and fix those, not everything.
- Below 60: Redo. The pack does not yet evidence the engagement, whatever the build quality was.
