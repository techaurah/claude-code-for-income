# Solution for Lesson 31: Capstone delivery: the pack and the self-grading rubric

The worked grading of the course's own capstone pack, scored from the evidence in Lessons 29 and 30:

- Scope fidelity: 3. Every scope line in the brief is delivered and traceable, and the exclusions are restated in the brief and discovery notes.
- Price integrity: 3. The build-up sums to $1,850, the floor parts sum to $720, and the assumptions are printed in engagement.json next to the numbers.
- QA evidence: 3. All 11 checks are recorded per check, the Q07 opt-out failure carries its root cause, its stop-condition fix, and its retest, and the gate includes checks that protect people, not only features.
- Handover completeness: 2. The handover notes, runbook, and limits are specified in the pack, but the pack as shipped in this course does not evidence a walked training session with the client team, so the descriptor for 3 is not met.
- Honesty of numbers: 3. Every figure is the labelled worked example with assumptions printed, and Lesson 30 states plainly what the numbers do not show.
- Support readiness: 2. The 30 day window is scoped and priced, and the retainer path is described, but the first report cycle is not yet scheduled in the pack, which the rubric's top descriptor requires.

Weighted total: scope 3 of 3 at weight 0.20, price 3 at 0.15, QA 3 at 0.20, handover 2 at 0.15, honesty 3 at 0.20, support 2 at 0.10. That is 0.20 plus 0.15 plus 0.20 plus 0.10 plus 0.20 plus 0.0667 of the maximum on the two 2 scores, giving 91.7 percent. Verdict: ship it.

The weaknesses, named as the lesson requires: handover evidence and support scheduling are the two soft spots, and both are pack gaps rather than build gaps. The fix list is short and specific: record the training session when it happens and add its receipt to the pack, and schedule the first retainer report date before the support window opens. A pack can ship at 91.7 with named weaknesses. That is the standard the course asks you to keep: the grade is high because the evidence is strong, and the weaknesses are printed because they are real.
