# Solution for Lesson 01: The honest economics: where the money actually is

A worked classification, using a website chatbot build, the category the platform data shows growing at 71%:

**Before.** "I will build you an AI chatbot for your website." Almost everything in that sentence is simple-end: the widget, the scripted answers, the embed code. An agent produces all of it quickly, so does every competitor's agent, and the per-contract price trend in the data is what happens next.

**After.** "I will connect an assistant to your booking system and your price list, teach it your cancellation policy, gate anything it cannot answer to a human, and hand you a weekly report of what visitors asked that your site failed to answer." The complex parts are now the product: integration with a real system of record, policy judgement encoded and tested, an escalation boundary, and reporting that creates the next piece of work. The agent still types the widget in minutes; the client is paying for the parts the agent cannot be accountable for. That rewrite, positioning the same skill at the end of the market where earnings rose, is the whole exercise, and it is the move Modules 4 to 7 repeat at four different scales.
