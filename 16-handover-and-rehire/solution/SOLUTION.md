# Solution for Lesson 16: Handover: documentation, training, and the rehire

The shipped `lumen_record.json` generates a complete pack: four deliverables for the Lumen Med Spa enquiry response system, run instructions with a health check and a one minute test, a change guide that names config edits for hours and prices and requires tests after changing the handoff rule, support terms with a 30 day defect window and one business day replies, and a next problem, no shows after booking, observed in the build's own follow up data.

Removing support terms and next problem produces a report naming exactly those two sections, and nothing else, which is how a completeness check should behave. A sample training outline for this pack: 15 minutes walking an enquiry to a booking on live test data, 15 minutes with the front desk running the flow, 15 minutes recovering a stopped service from the runbook, and 15 minutes on boundaries, support, and questions.
