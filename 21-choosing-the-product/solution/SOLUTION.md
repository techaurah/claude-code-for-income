# Solution for Lesson 21: Micro-SaaS: choosing a problem worth a product

The fixture ranking is the worked solution. No-show reminders for clinics win at 22 because the evidence is structural: the pain is countable in empty slots, the frequency is daily, the buyers already pay for booking software, and the reminder rules barely change once set. Nothing in that score requires optimism, which is what a good score looks like.

The invoice helper survives at 18 as the honest middle: real pain, real frequency, thinner ability to pay, and no audience yet. It is buildable second, or first if the distribution note improves through a partner.

The tax filing idea is the teaching case. At 15 it outscores many ideas builders happily start, and the kill line still buries it for maintenance lightness at 1. Annual rule changes across jurisdictions, plus liability when the agent is wrong, mean the tail is not a cost line. It is the product. Killing it here costs one JSON entry. Building it first would cost the quarter Lesson 22 is about to spend on something better.
