# Solution for Lesson 14: Pricing the work: fixed, anchored, and defensible

WORKED EXAMPLE, with its assumptions printed. Lumen Med Spa, fictional. Human time 9 hours at $75 is $675, plan share $25, measured API cost $20, so the floor is $720. The fixed price is $1,850, built up in Lesson 29. Gross margin is $1,850 minus $720, which is $1,130 before platform fee.

On a 10 percent channel the same price delivers $1,665, the margin after fee is $945, and the fee adjusted floor is $800, the price below which the channel version of this job loses money even before profit. Against the client's stated anchor of $2,400 a month for the cost of slow replies, the one off price sits inside a single month of the problem. That is the whole defensible structure: floor below, anchor above, fee applied, terms stated, and no number in the chain invented by hope.
