# Visual guide

Every diagram is generated by `generate_visuals.py` in this
folder; rerun it to regenerate the set. Diagrams carry structures
and labelled worked examples only. No chart in this course plots
an income outcome as if it were data, per the honesty rules in
the root README.

- `course-map.svg`: the ten modules and their lesson ranges.
- `workstation-stack.svg`: the five workstation layers from
  Lesson 02 to Lesson 06, human judgement on top, hook gates
  underneath.
- `unattended-modes.svg`: the four ways to run Claude Code
  unattended, compared in Lesson 08.
- `cost-stack.svg`: the three payment routes with list prices
  checked October 2026 and the break-even rule, from Lesson 10.
- `income-tracks.svg`: the four income tracks of Modules 4 to 7
  and how they differ in buyer and cycle.
- `delivery-pipeline.svg`: Module 4's delivery spine, with the
  QA gate in amber.
- `capstone-flow.svg`: the capstone engagement from brief to
  self-grading rubric, Lessons 29 to 31.
