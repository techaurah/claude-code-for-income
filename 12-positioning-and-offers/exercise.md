# Exercise for Lesson 12: Positioning: selling outcomes, not hours

Take the rewritten service from your Lesson 01 exercise and write a full canvas JSON for it: buyer, pain, outcome, system, proof, and boundary, each in the buyer's plain language. Render it with `offer_canvas.py`. Then write a second canvas for the same skill aimed at a different buyer, and note what had to change in the outcome and the proof. Finally, deliberately submit a canvas with the buyer "businesses" and read the refusal: that refusal is the standard your own drafts must pass before a buyer ever sees them.
