# Solution for Lesson 12: Positioning: selling outcomes, not hours

The shipped `lumen_offer.json` is the worked canvas. Its buyer, owners of multi location med spas, passes the word minimum because it names a role, a setting, and a scale. Its outcome, every enquiry answered within one minute at all hours, is painful, checkable, and priced by the buyer's own overnight problem. Its proof names inspectable assets, a demo, a QA record, and a runbook, not adjectives.

Retargeting the same enquiry system at a solo clinic changes more than the buyer line. The outcome shrinks to cover for one front desk, the system loses the multi location routing, and the proof that matters becomes the handover pack, because a solo operator fears being stranded with a system they cannot run. Same skill, different offer: that is positioning doing its work.
