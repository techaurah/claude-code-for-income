# Solution for Lesson 26: Retainers: structure, reporting, and renewal

The Lumen month-one report renders with 212 enquiries handled, a median first response of 2 minutes, and 94 percent answered within 5 minutes. The measured maintenance total is $13.50 for the month (6.40 plus 4.10 plus 3.00), printed in the report rather than estimated. Both incidents carry resolutions, both changes sit inside the agreed scope, and the scope check passes clean: all delivered items fall inside the agreed scope.

The teaching point is the doctored case. Add a change tagged "advertising" (a scope the Lumen agreement never included) and the report flags it by name in the scope check section, with the reminder that flagged work needs a conversation: bill it, swap it, or amend the scope in writing. A retainer run this way has no silent months and no surprise arguments, which is most of what renewal actually is.
