# Solution for Lesson 17: Productized services: choosing an offer that repeats

The fixture ranking is the worked solution, and it is worth reading slowly. The enquiry response package for med spas scores 24 of 25: buyer 5, pain 5, scope 5, repeatability 5, and a support load of 2, which inverts to 4. Every high score there is checkable. The buyer exists in the capstone as Lumen Med Spa. The pain is enquiries arriving when nobody is on shift. The scope fits on one page in Lesson 19. The delivery is the pipeline Lesson 18 builds.

The bespoke migration scores 13. Its pain is real, which is why pain urgency is 4, but scope and repeatability sit at 2 because the next migration will not resemble this one. The correct conclusion is not to drop migrations. It is to keep selling them as discovery-led bespoke work, where the price can move with the scope.

The generic chatbot scores 8 and is flagged. The flag matters more than the total: buyer clarity at 1 means every later system, the page, the pipeline, the support window, has nothing stable to attach to. Productizing it would not create a product. It would create a fixed price for unlimited interpretation, which is how productized offers die in their first month.
